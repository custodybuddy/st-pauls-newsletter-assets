01 / Pastoral Note

Greetings Friends

We hope you have enjoyed this lovely summer and were able to find some refreshment in whatever ways you enjoy. And, just like that, fall is upon us!

As we continue our newsletter series, this fall, we would like to focus on another ministry at St. Paul’s — our Compassionate Care Ministry.

Our Mission

We are a Christ-centered, worshipping community serving and leading people to Jesus.

02 / St. Paul’s Ministry Spotlight

Compassionate Care Ministry

Sharing Compassion

In our Vision Statements, or the strategies we want to use to fulfill our Mission, #6 says, “Hurting people find hope and healing in the compassion of Jesus”. Throughout the Bible, we are called to care for one another. So, the focus of this ministry is to discern how we can care for all ages in our congregation and community, using the spiritual gifts that God has given us.

We know so many people who are struggling to find hope today, especially when the challenges come. Jesus is God’s compassionate response to our helpless situation in life. We are invited to follow Jesus and let Jesus display his compassion in us to a hurting world.

Vision Statement #6

“Hurting people find hope and healing in the compassion of Jesus.”

03 / Care in the community

A ministry of care

We are aware that there are hurting people in our church, our neighbourhoods and in our workplaces, and God is calling us to reach out and care for them. This has been at the heart of our Compassionate Response Ministry, where we have offered care and support to those we know who are facing a challenge in their life. We want to remind them they are not alone. As a church, we want to continue to grow this ministry.
