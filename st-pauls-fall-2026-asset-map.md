## Mapping notes

- The preferred hero is `fall_2026_hero_greetings`, with a PNG original and JPEG delivery copy. The older v2 URL is a compatibility alias for the new artwork; its former bytes are preserved in `fall-2026-hero-greetings-banner-clean-v2-original.jpeg`.
- `upcoming-events-portrait.png` was renamed to describe its actual landscape dimensions, 1672 × 941.
- Both event-title WebPs have a checkerboard baked into RGB pixels. They are not transparent; use the separate transparent PNG when transparency is needed.
- The divider, prayer heading and landscape event heading retain their PNG alpha channels.
- No image background removal, cropping or artwork regeneration was performed. JPEG copies change encoding only.
- No private preview URLs, upload identifiers or local source paths are included in this public mapping.
