Did You Know?

Our Prayer Shawl Ministry

For many years, a group of faithful knitters and crocheters have created beautiful prayer shawls that are taken to those who are facing a trial. We have heard so many messages of gratitude for these beautiful gifts of care. We want to continue to offer this ministry.

The Power of Prayer

We have a Prayer Team that prays for those individuals and situations that are raised during our weekly worship service. They also pray, with sensitivity and confidentiality, for those concerns that Pastor Al may become aware of during the week. We believe in the power of prayer and have seen answers to these prayers.
