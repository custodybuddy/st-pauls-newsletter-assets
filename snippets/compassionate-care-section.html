Compassionate Care Ministry

Caring like Christ. Walking together in love.

At St. Paul’s, we believe that caring for one another is at the heart of following Jesus. Our Compassionate Care Ministry surrounds people with Christ’s love during life’s joys and challenges—offering prayer, encouragement, practical help, and a listening ear.

Whether it’s supporting someone through illness, celebrating a new baby, visiting a shut-in, or walking with a family in difficulty, we are here to remind one another that we are never alone.
